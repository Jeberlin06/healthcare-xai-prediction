Understanding Explainable AI (XAI) in Healthcare

Why Explainability Matters

Traditional black-box machine learning models deliver high predictive accuracy but offer little insight into why a decision was made. In clinical environments, understanding feature attribution is critical for establishing medical trust and supporting doctor decision-making.

SHAP (Shapley Additive exPlanations)

Grounded in cooperative game theory, SHAP calculates the exact additive contribution of each biomarker toward the final risk output, guaranteeing consistency and local accuracy.

LIME (Local Surrogate Models)

LIME approximates the black-box model locally around a specific patient's data point by fitting an interpretable sparse linear decision boundary.
