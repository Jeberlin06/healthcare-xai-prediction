Help & Frequently Asked Questions

How does the XGBoost model calculate disease risk?

The model takes clinical observation inputs and passes them through trained decision tree ensembles to evaluate log-odds risk probabilities against threshold baselines.

Is my uploaded batch CSV data kept secure?

Yes, all batch inference requests are processed locally in-memory on your server instance and are not shared with external third parties.

Can I export PDF reports for patient records?

Yes, navigate to the Doctor Report tab and click Print / Save PDF to export complete clinical summaries.
